My second sprite to draw is Ninetales, which is an up in difficulty compared to Bulbasaur. I did not finish it as it feels I've spent way too long on this. For a newbie like me, drawing like this takes over 2.5 hours. Pixel Art most definitely is not easy.

Blob. With Bulbasaur, I started with an attempt at lineart instead of from a blob of sillhouette. This makes work considerably easier for me, especially as I haven't been good at lineart. One lesson to learn here is that I was focused on the *blob* part instead of the sillhouette part. Thus, when I try to sculpt it into concrete shape, I need to do a lot of adjustments to make the subshapes look proper and not-stiff. The circular tail-folds took me quite a while; the head side profile is rather flat; and the mane is just stiff.

Curves. I had a bit of difficulty making the curves resemble the reference. As above, this was on the tail-folds and the mane. What I found out is that making curves look good takes time (at least for an inexperienced person like me). I should also prepare in advance when drawing the sillhouette which shape will curve, and how curvy they would be. In the middle of my struggling, I recall a tip that when drawing lines, a segment should not be neighbored by two segments of bigger size. If a neighbor is bigger, then the other should be as big or smaller as the target pixel. Very useful!

Color. Looking at the reference, I am amazed at while the coloring looks straightforward, naively trying to replicate it (without eyedropping the reference) is quite hard. I think I've spent over 20 minutes just playing with different colors. One thing is that the reference is subtly vibrant! There's this beautiful balanced point between the dull, grayish-end and orange. Eventually, I gave up and used Aseprite's saturation slider. Kind of a shortcut, but it works better than handpicking colors. Still not as good as the reference, though!
