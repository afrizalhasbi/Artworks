Next on my list is Krabby. I initially thought that I'd rather draw Krabby than Dodrio, because it looks to be just a lil' guy. But it turns out Krabby is more difficult to draw than I expected.

Once again, I started with a sketch -> fill approach as I did for Dodrio. this looks quite acceptable for the claws and legs, I feel, but the problem is that Krabby's design is actually really compact.

Shape. It's a mix of a very well-tuned sillhouette and lineart. Krabby's body is very compact. The under-armor looks like a tight fit, but it also doubles as a mouth. More importantly, the way it is drawn in the reference, it very much looks like a shield! This was my problem. I had focused too much on the angling of the claws and underestimated the underarmor, instead having it become a rough blobby shape. Moreover, the top/head part is actually small and narrow, making it a great contrast with the under-armor shape.

Lineart. Krabby has a surprisingly intricate lineart. Because the body is very compact, the decisions of the line angles and positioning for the legs matter tremendously in accentuating the shape. It's also a bit of a struggle for me to get the colors for the legs correct, so I ended up with this stiff-looking color scheme for the outlines.
