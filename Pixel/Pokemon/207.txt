Gligar is next on the list, and I do find myself enjoying drawing it quite a bit, maybe because it doesn't turn out as bad as I expected. I had some trouble with the pincers, and it definitely doesn't look as chompy as I wanted. And frankly I can't fully pinpoint *why* it doesn't work so well. The reference simply have a much better grasp on shapes and pixel tuning, and stuff like this makes it very clear that Pokemon sprites are AAA quality.

Of course, the clearly bad part is the face. It doesn't look as whimsical; sort of more devilish is what my version look like, but not in a good way. There's a lot of charm in the original face, which may be a combination of head proportions, eye shapes, individual pixel tuning of the tongue and teeth, and the ears too; essentially, I feel like the face is very difficult to get just that one specific look right!

But the good thing: The body actually looks decent! The torso, legs, and tails, flaps, I'm frankly quite happy with them. I think I really am getting better slowly but surely
