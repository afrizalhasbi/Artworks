This is by far the absolute worst Pokemon pixel art I have drawn so far. It's really quite incredible. Everything about it is rotten: proportions, shapes, comprehensibility. I truly hate this piece of garbage, and I went in thinking that drawing this would be easy and straightforward, probably because Ledyba/Ledian aren't great Pokemons. But here I am completely broken by trying to draw this. I stopped after around an hour because I felt this drawing is unsalvageable, and I'm better off wrapping up a crappy drawing and taking lessons as opposed to making it look half-decent.


Fitting shapes. What I have learnt from Krabby and Meganium is to get an understanding of the "primitive" shape of what I am trying to draw. We have Krabby's underarmor and Meganium's stacked triangles as flowers. So with Ledyba, I caught on immediately that the mouth/jaw and the head is almost exactly the same family of shape as Krabby's underarmor. But then why did it have to look so ugly? Certainly I did not tune the shapes enough. The head looks a little too wide on the right. It's also a bit unclear if the top-side of the head is in front or behind the jaw. But I did need to resize the head using aseprite's drag tool, so this may have nontrivial effect on how ugly it looks, but I doubt it'll look good if I did not resize it.

Round shapes. Both the head and the body are just round circular shapes, yet they both look horrendous. I started again with the blob -> outline sketch methods, but it did not remotely look cute, while the original sprite does. I think I really should start drawing round pixel art shapes using a Loomis-like initial sketch. Which is strange that I didn't do, because looking back, I did draw Bulbasaur's bulb partly by drawing contours.

Proportions. I am simply stumped by making the proportions of the head and the body correct. Perhaps though the problem is me trying to draw on an empty part of the canvas and dragging it over the neck. It turns out it was too small, so I had to resize it to be bigger, but it ruined the shape of the head and my fixes are choppy.
