Two things are a bit difficult with Meganium:

Round flowers. The flowers in the original references are well-shaped and round. In fact, it is quite imaginable that the the flower is 2-layered: first is a downward-pointing triangle, which acts as a base, and the second is the round ones on top of it, the ones with the yellow markings. I realized this much too late, only when I realized my imitation did not at all match the reference. This is, in fact, the same mistake I did as when I was drawing Krabby. I did not realize that the under-armor forms a shield-like attachment, and the results became blobby; I also missed to understand the actual shape that this flowers really are. It's a very instructive lesson.
